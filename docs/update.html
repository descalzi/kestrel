Update your KestrelOne

Updates install straight from this page over USB. Nothing to download and no tools to install: your browser talks to the device directly, and your settings and WiFi credentials are kept.

Updating needs Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop computer. This browser cannot talk to USB devices; Safari, Firefox, phones and tablets cannot update KestrelOne.

  1. Connect Plug KestrelOne into this computer with a USB-C cable. It must be a data cable — a charge-only lead powers the device but the browser will not see it.
  2. Check what it runs Optional: compare your device with the latest release.
    Latest release Checking… What's new
    Your device Not checked yet

  3. Update Press the button and choose the KestrelOne port. Your browser usually lists it as USB JTAG/serial debug unit. This browser cannot talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer — Safari and Firefox do not support it, and nor do phones or tablets. Serial access was blocked. This page must be opened over HTTPS.

    If the installer offers Erase device, leave it unticked: it wipes your settings and WiFi names. The update takes a minute or so; keep KestrelOne plugged in until it says it is done. When it restarts, this page reads it back and shows the result in step 2.

  4. Reconnect Join the KestrelOne WiFi again from your phone or tablet. The default password is kestrelone.

Nothing is uploaded or stored: the check only reads the device's own startup message over USB.

What's new

0.7.0

4 October 2026
  • A simpler device page. It opens on Traffic with a one-line answer to "is it working?", plain tiles for aircraft, tablet, GPS and battery, and the traffic display. Settings keeps only what an owner needs. The detailed engineering views are still there, behind a Hacker mode switch at the bottom of Settings.
  • Dark or light, your choice. A button in the top bar switches between automatic, dark and light, whatever the phone itself is set to.
  • Support for KestrelOne v2 boards, which set their own receiver threshold instead of relying on a factory adjustment, and offer a Receiver sensitivity setting. Leave it at 0 unless you have a reason to change it.

0.6.0

22 September 2026
  • Steadier altitude in SkyDemon. Traffic and your own position now go directly to each connected tablet instead of being broadcast to the whole network, which stops a brief "altitude invalid" flicker.
  • Aircraft on the ground stay on the map between their less frequent ground reports, including those that do not report an altitude.
  • A momentary gap in the GPS timing signal no longer marks your position invalid.
  • On the device's own page, Status and Traffic are now one tab, and PilotAware ground stations it hears are listed on the 868 tab.

If something goes wrong

The browser does not list any serial port

Almost always the cable. Many USB-C leads carry power only; the device will light up but never appear to the computer. Try a different cable before anything else. If it still does not appear, unplug it, wait a few seconds and plug it back in.

The update failed part-way through

Nothing is broken. The device keeps its previous firmware until a new one is written completely, and an interrupted write can simply be run again. Unplug it, plug it back in, and press Update once more.

It updated, but my EFB no longer sees traffic

Rejoin the KestrelOne WiFi network from your tablet — phones and tablets usually drop back to a remembered network while the device restarts. Then open its own page at 192.168.4.1: the line at the top of the Traffic tab says whether it is receiving and whether your tablet is connected.

How do I see which version my device is running?

Plug it in and press Check my device above. If that cannot read it, join its WiFi and open 192.168.4.1: the version is under Settings → Firmware, and at the bottom of the first tab (called Status on firmware before 0.7.0).

Can I go back to an older version?

Yes, though this page only ever offers the latest. Older releases are kept and can be flashed with the same tool if you need one. Email martin.descalzi@gmail.com rather than guessing, so we can work out what went wrong with the new one.
