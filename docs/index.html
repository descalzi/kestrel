What it hears

Airliners and ADS-B-equipped aircraft today, with the gliding and paragliding world arriving as the decoders are finished.

ADS-B Position, altitude, callsign and climb rate on 1090 MHz
Mode S Transponder replies from aircraft without ADS-B
FLARM & OGN soon Gliders, tugs and the Open Glider Network
PilotAware soon The P3I network, widely flown in UK GA
FANET soon Paragliders and hang gliders
ADS-L soon The open EASA-backed standard

KestrelOne is a receiver, so it works alongside your transponder rather than replacing it — that is still what makes you visible to others. It shows what it can hear, which is never quite everything, so it belongs beside a good lookout rather than instead of one.

What you get

Charge it, switch it on, connect your tablet. No installation, no wiring, no subscription.

Traffic on the map you already use

Standard GDL90 over its own WiFi network — the same format commercial receivers use. SkyDemon and other flight apps pick it up with no cables and nothing to configure.

Knows where you are

Built-in satellite positioning and a barometer establish your own position and pressure altitude, so nearby traffic is shown as height above or below you rather than as a number to work out.

Grab it and go

Runs from its own battery and charges over USB-C. Nothing wired into the aircraft, nothing to certify, and it moves between aircraft as easily as your headset.

Install firmware

Firmware installs straight from this page over USB. Nothing to download, no tools to set up.

  1. ConnectPlug KestrelOne into this computer with a USB-C cable.
  2. InstallPress the button below and choose the KestrelOne serial port.
  3. Connect to itWhen it finishes, join the KestrelOne WiFi network from your phone or tablet.
This browser cannot talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer — Safari and Firefox do not support it, and nor do phones or tablets. Serial access was blocked. This page must be opened over HTTPS.

Ticking Erase device during install clears saved settings and WiFi credentials along with the firmware. Leave it unticked to keep them.
